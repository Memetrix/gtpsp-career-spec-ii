This is the first public beta of **GT Career: Spec II**. It adds a nine-hall career to Gran Turismo PSP, with connected championships, persistent tuning, prize cars, and segmented endurance races. The new Spec II cover was checked on PSP Go.

Download `GT-Career-SpecII-v0.27.2-beta.1.zip` below. It contains the patch installer, instructions, source snapshot, licences, and ten unscaled 480×272 gameplay screenshots. **It does not contain a game ISO or save file.** You need your own unmodified USA UCUS98632 v2.00 ISO and the dependencies listed in [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/Memetrix/gtpsp-career-spec-ii/blob/main/INSTALL.md).

The release ZIP was extracted and its patch installer reproduced the finished ISO byte for byte from the clean original. The v0.27.2 gameplay volume has been played on PSP Go; beta.1 keeps that volume byte-identical. The beta.1 cover was checked on PSP Go. A full accumulated 24-hour endurance run and other PSP models remain unverified. The two Hidden Course Test events are experimental and may fail. See [known beta limits](https://github.com/Memetrix/gtpsp-career-spec-ii/blob/main/INSTALL.md#known-beta-limits) before playing.
